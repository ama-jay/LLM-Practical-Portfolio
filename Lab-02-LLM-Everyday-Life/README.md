### **Lab 02 – AI in everyday challenges**



#### **Group Activity – The Plastic Problem**



***Group: 6***



***Product Name: EcoReward***



***Target Users: University students***



* Students regularly use single use plastic cups, bottles, food containers and shopping bags. Our challenge was to develop an innovative product that encourages students to reduce plastic waste.
* EcoReward is a student ID based reward system that encourages students to reduce single use plastic consumption through positive incentives.
* Students earn EcoPoints by completing verified environmentally friendly actions on campus. The accumulated points can be redeemed for rewards such as campus credit.



**System Workflow**



1. Student scans their university ID.
2. The environmentally friendly action is verified.
3. EcoPoints are added to the student's account.
4. Points can be redeemed for rewards.



**Reward System**



Students earn EcoPoints by completing verified plastic saving actions, such as using reusable cups, refilling water bottles and choosing plastic free packaging. The accumulated points can be redeemed for rewards such as campus credit. No points are awarded for purchasing single use plastics or being inactive(not attend campus), ensuring that rewards encourage genuine environmentally friendly behaviour.



Ex:  Use a reusable coffee cup  +5 , 
Purchase a single use plastic bottle  0



**LLM Integration in EcoReward**



The proposed system includes an AI powered Eco Assistant that provides,



* Personalized recommendations based on student activity.
* Explanations of how EcoPoints are earned.
* Weekly summaries of environmental progress.
* Suggestions to improve sustainable habits.



**Challenges**



Concept put 0 rewards to students who did not attend university, so they not receive rewards without actively reducing plastic consumption.
